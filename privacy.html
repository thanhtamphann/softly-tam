Privacy & Editorial Policy

This is a personal publication built around honesty, care, and respect for the people who read it.

Information and privacy

TAMORA does not currently collect newsletter subscriptions or contact-form messages. Readers may choose to contact the author through the social channels shown on the website.

Analytics

TAMORA currently uses Google Analytics 4 to measure site visits and article engagement, including reading-progress events. This helps improve the publication and understand which stories readers find useful.

Editorial approach

Articles combine personal reflection, independent research, and practical ideas. Personal experiences are presented as experiences—not universal answers. Sources should be credited wherever research or third-party reporting materially informs an article.

Financial and mental-health content

Content about money, psychology, relationships, or wellbeing is for general education and personal reflection. It does not replace advice from a qualified financial, medical, legal, or mental-health professional.

Affiliate links and partnerships

There are currently no affiliate links or sponsored articles on this website. Any future commercial relationship will be disclosed clearly near the relevant content.

Contact

Readers can use the social channels shown on TAMORA for corrections, collaboration enquiries, or other questions.

Last updated: September 14, 2026. This policy should be reviewed whenever analytics, advertising, affiliate, newsletter, or other site services change.
